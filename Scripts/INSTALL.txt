TradeSync — How to Install
==========================

Requirements: macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later. Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.


1. INSTALL
----------
1. Unzip the file you were sent (double-click it).
2. Drag TradeSync.app into your Applications folder.


2. OPEN IT THE FIRST TIME
-------------------------
TradeSync isn't distributed through the App Store, so macOS asks you to
confirm it the first time. You only do this once.

1. Double-click TradeSync in Applications. macOS shows a warning — click Done
   (or Cancel).
2. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security.
3. Scroll down to the Security section. Next to the message about TradeSync,
   click "Open Anyway", then confirm with your password or Touch ID.
4. TradeSync opens. From now on it launches normally.

On macOS 14 (Sonoma) you can instead right-click TradeSync.app, choose Open,
then click Open.

If macOS says the app "is damaged and can't be opened", open Terminal and run:
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/TradeSync.app
then open it again.


3. GETTING STARTED
------------------
- The app opens with sample data so you can look around. Remove it from the
  banner on the Dashboard once your own trades are in.
- Your name on the welcome screen comes from your Mac account. Change it in
  Settings > Startup.
- Add your trading accounts in Settings > Accounts > Add Account.


4. CONNECTING A PROP FIRM ACCOUNT ON TRADOVATE (Tradeify, Apex, etc.)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Tradovate doesn't allow apps to read prop firm or evaluation accounts
directly, so TradeSync reads Tradovate's own trade report instead.

1. In TradeSync: Settings > Add Account. Set the firm (e.g. Tradeify) and the
   connection to "Tradovate". Leave the account number blank — it fills in
   from your first export. Enter your commission per contract if you know it.
2. In Tradovate: click your account name at the top, then the small gear icon
   to open Account Reports. Choose the ORDERS tab (not Performance), pick your
   account and date range, click Go, then Download CSV.
3. The file lands in your Downloads folder and TradeSync logs the trades within
   a minute: entry, exit, stop loss, take profit, contracts and P&L.
4. The first time, macOS asks whether TradeSync can access your Downloads
   folder. Click Allow — otherwise it can't see your exports.

After each session, export the Orders report for that day and your trades
appear in the Daily Journal, ready for your screenshots and notes.


5. CONNECTING A METATRADER 5 ACCOUNT (FTMO, FundedNext, MT5 brokers)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Settings > Add Account > set the connection to "MetaTrader 5".
MT5 syncing goes through MetaApi (metaapi.cloud), a third-party service with a
free tier. Create an account there, add your MT5 login (the read-only investor
password is enough), then paste the API token and account ID into TradeSync.
Trades then import automatically, including stop loss and take profit.


YOUR DATA
---------
Everything stays on your own Mac, in:
   ~/Library/Application Support/TradeSync/
Nothing is uploaded or shared.
